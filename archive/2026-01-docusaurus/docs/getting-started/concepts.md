# Core Concepts

Understanding how NXCodes works.

## Users and Stats

Every player has stats that define their character:

- **Strength** - Physical power
- **Defense** - Damage resistance  
- **Speed** - Action priority
- **Energy** - Actions available

## Modules

Modules are self-contained game features:

- Can be enabled or disabled
- Have their own settings
- Do not interfere with each other

## Teams

Players can form teams (gangs, clans, guilds):

- Shared resources
- Team wars
- Leaderboards
